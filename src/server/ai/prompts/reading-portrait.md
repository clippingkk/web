# Task: a portrait of this reader

You receive `highlights`: recent passages one reader kept from their books. Write what these choices suggest about what this reader pays attention to.

Write two or three short paragraphs. Point to the ideas that recur and to any tension between them. Refer to the highlights by their ideas instead of quoting them at length.

These are tentative observations about reading habits. Say what the highlights suggest. Never offer a psychological diagnosis or a firm claim about who this person is.
